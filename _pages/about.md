# About Me

I am an undergraduate researcher in **Artificial Intelligence**, studying in the Advanced Program in Computer Science (APCS) at the [University of Science, VNU-HCM](https://hcmus.edu.vn/). I am a Research Assistant at **SeLAB** under [Dr. Trung-Nghia Le](https://ltnghia.github.io/), and I am a research collaborator with [Dr. Thanh-Toan Do](https://research.monash.edu/en/persons/toan-do/) at **Monash University**. I also work as an AI Engineer at **FPT Telecom**.

My research studies how **vision-language models** can understand and generate visual content under fine-grained, multimodal control. Recent work includes a benchmark for sketch–text composed image retrieval ([ACM ICMR 2026](/publications/VietFashion/)), training-free style personalization for visual autoregressive models ([SoICT 2026](/publications/CoPFB/)), and MLLM fine-tuning for fashion retrieval ([SoICT 2025, Best Poster Award](/publications/ExploringFashionRetrieval/)).
